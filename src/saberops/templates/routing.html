Add model

Only discovery-verified, fresh models with an exact executable binding can enter routing. A registered adapter alone is not enough: the binding must resolve through the canonical registry. Adding a model outside the static registry records its non-secret discovery evidence and exact binding identity as owner approval; it never enrolls automatically, and eligibility is evaluated in each context (an unknown training requirement fails closed in training-denied contexts).
