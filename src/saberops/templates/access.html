Model Access

User-owned account and API access. This page answers HOW an already-selected binding connects — it never selects models or routes work. Adding a provider makes its models available for selection; it does not place them into routing. Saving a connection runs one bounded discovery automatically; otherwise use “Refresh models”. A backend that cannot enumerate models reports that truthfully instead of inventing a list.
